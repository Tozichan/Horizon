### [Neuralink 获 FDA 批准开展 ALS 关键试验](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/neuralink-gets-clearance-for-brain-implant-trial-in-als-patients?srnd=phx-technology) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Neuralink 表示，已获得 FDA 批准，将在美国启动一项名为“LINK”的植入式脑机接口关键临床试验，面向渐冻症（ALS）患者招募，目标是把大脑信号直接转化为计算机控制指令。该公司称，这是美国首个获批的此类关键试验，试验数据将作为未来申请医疗器械上市许可的核心依据。Neuralink 还表示，其早期研究已在全球为超过 30 名瘫痪患者植入设备。上述信息来自公司声明及单一来源的简要报道，尚无独立验证或试验设计细节。

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 26, 07:15

**「Background」** Neuralink&\#x27;s earlier human studies were feasibility and safety investigations; the company says it has implanted its device in more than 30 paralyzed patients worldwide through that early research. A pivotal trial is the later-stage study whose data regulators use to decide whether to grant marketing approval, so the LINK clearance moves the program from safety validation toward that approval pathway. Neuralink&\#x27;s own announcement describes LINK as the first pivotal trial for an implantable brain-computer interface in the US, and reporting on the clearance notes the device remains investigational during enrollment.

**「影响」** 对 ALS 患者而言，这意味着在关键试验阶段可能获得参与植入式脑机接口研究的机会，但招募条件、规模和时间表尚未披露。对 Neuralink 而言，关键试验数据将直接支撑其后续上市申请，因此试验能否按计划推进将决定其商业化路径。

### [Nvidia Reportedly Pauses AI Server Shipments to Four Southeast Asian Countries](https://www.ft.com/content/7838a715-07d4-4605-aa60-21f5af2b2370) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Nvidia has reportedly asked major manufacturing partners to pause AI server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, according to the Financial Times. Dell, Supermicro and other vendors that assemble Nvidia chips into complete systems have told buyers in the four countries that the orders cannot leave the factory. Three people familiar with the matter linked the freeze to a new round of U.S. export controls, though it remains unclear whether the measure is temporary. The report says the U.S. Commerce Department issued new rules last week requiring regional cloud providers to obtain export licenses within 90 days and to verify that customers do not include restricted Chinese entities; the details and duration of the shipment pause are unconfirmed.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 26, 07:38

**「Background」** The reported pause follows a U.S. Commerce Department rule, described in the source as issued the previous week, requiring regional cloud service providers to obtain export licenses within 90 days and verify that customers do not include restricted Chinese entities. Earlier reporting indicated Commerce was preparing a rule aimed at stopping Chinese AI companies from remotely accessing advanced AI chips through overseas data centers \(tool-2-1\). The four Southeast Asian countries have served as transshipment points because Nvidia&\#x27;s most advanced processors cannot be exported directly to mainland China.

**「Impact」** The four countries have served as transit points through which Chinese AI companies obtain Nvidia&\#x27;s most advanced processors, which cannot be exported directly to mainland China. If the pause holds, buyers in these markets and the assemblers handling their orders face delays, and regional cloud providers would need to secure export licenses and verify customers under the new 90-day requirement.

### [Zhipu and Moonshot flagship model releases reportedly delayed](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-chip-curbs-distillation-crackdown-threaten-chinese-ai-model-development-2026-11-26/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A brief report citing Reuters says Zhipu&\#x27;s planned flagship GLM-5.5, described as roughly 3 trillion parameters, has not shipped as expected this month and may slip to the first quarter of 2027 or later, with people familiar citing increasingly effective anti-distillation defenses. The report also says Moonshot AI internally expects its major release cadence to stretch from six months to more than a year if it loses overseas compute entirely, potentially pushing the next flagship Kimi K4 to mid-2027; it attributes training-data difficulty to anti-distillation measures from Anthropic and OpenAI and says the labs have turned to distilling Google and Meta models. The current report extends that thread by tying anti-distillation defenses and overseas compute access to specific flagship release timelines at Zhipu and Moonshot.

**「Why it matters」** If the reported timelines hold, users and developers planning around GLM-5.5 or Kimi K4 would face waits of several months to over a year, and the labs&\#x27; shift toward distilling Google and Meta models would change which upstream models their outputs depend on. ### [荷兰大臣称不接受美国限制 ASML 对华销售的法案](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-26/netherlands-plans-to-fight-rollout-of-proposed-us-curbs-on-asml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

荷兰外贸与发展合作大臣 Sjoerd Sjoerdsma 于 11 月 26 日在议会表示，荷兰不会接受美国进一步限制 ASML 对华销售，并反对 MATCH Act 的域外管辖效力。该法案由美国两党共同提出，要求荷兰、日本等盟友对华实施更严格的出口管制，否则美方将单方面限制其半导体设备；中国约占 ASML 今年销售额的 20%。

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 26, 14:56

**「Background」** The MATCH Act, introduced in April 2026, would extend existing controls beyond ASML&\#x27;s most advanced EUV tools to include DUV immersion machines, which are currently exportable to China under a restricted licensing framework \(tool-1-2, tool-1-3\). It would require allies such as the Netherlands and Japan to adopt tougher China export controls or face unilateral US restrictions on their chip equipment \(tool-2-1, tool-2-3\).

**「Impact」** If the MATCH Act passes, ASML&\#x27;s China sales—about 20% of its revenue this year—could face tighter restrictions, affecting the company and its suppliers; the bill is still pending and the vote outcome is only expected.
