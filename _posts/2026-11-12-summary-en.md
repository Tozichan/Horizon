### [WorkflowArena Finds Strongest Model May Not Be Best Coordinator](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xt9hbe/would_you_pick_your_strongest_model_to_coordinate/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A Reddit post from Einsia describes an experiment in which Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra were given the same 32 challenging tasks and asked to write static workflows for four execution models, without editing the workspace themselves. Opus 5.5&\#x27;s workflows raised the executors&\#x27; continuous scores by 6.41 points on average versus letting the executors orchestrate themselves, while GPT-6 Astra&\#x27;s workflows lowered them by 2.53 points, even though Astra was stronger than all four executors on the main benchmark. The finding is part of WorkflowArena, the team&\#x27;s benchmark covering 145 long-horizon tasks across eight domains, with a linked paper and code repository. The post is a single team&\#x27;s account with limited methodological detail, so the result has not been independently verified.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/OliviaYii · Nov 11, 05:25

**「Background」** The post frames its experiment around a common multi-agent design: one model acts as coordinator, writing workflows and delegating subtasks to executor models that do the actual work. The reported comparison holds the 32 tasks and four executors fixed while swapping only the coordinator, which is what allows the workflow effect to be separated from the coordinator&\#x27;s own benchmark scores. WorkflowArena is presented as the team&\#x27;s broader benchmark of 145 long-horizon tasks across eight domains, where agents choose their own division of work and the final deliverable is checked against task requirements.

**「Impact」** For teams building mixed-model agent systems, the result suggests evaluating a candidate coordinator on the usefulness of the workflows it produces for the specific executors, rather than selecting it by its own benchmark score alone. The post asks builders how they currently assess this choice, noting the experiment used static workflows and 32 tasks.
